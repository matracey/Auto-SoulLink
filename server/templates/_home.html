Getting started

Choose your games and rules, connect both players, then play. The run board tracks your pairs as you go.

  1. Create a run.Pick a game family and the rules you want to play with. Both players use games from the same family.
  2. Prepare your cartridge.Take the run's cartridge download, or patch your own at /patcher. The companion patch is required, except for Yellow and the Emerald Expansion; a clean cartridge is refused.
  3. Connect both games.Download each player's setup from the run page. In BizHawk, load your game and save, then load the launcher in the Lua console.
  4. Start playing.Catch your first Pokémon in the same area. Check the board for linked pairs, your teams, and the run's recent events.
